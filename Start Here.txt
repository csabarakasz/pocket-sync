POCKET SYNC FOR MAC

1. Unzip the download and drag Pocket Sync.app to Applications or your Desktop.
2. Double-click Pocket Sync.app. The first launch needs internet access to
   install FFmpeg and Deno if they are missing.
3. Follow the walkthrough. You can reopen it from Help & settings in the sidebar.

If macOS says the developer can't be verified

This test build hasn't been notarized by Apple. Only proceed if you trust the
person you received it from and know the download hasn't been modified.

1. Try to open Pocket Sync.app once and dismiss the warning.
2. Open Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to Security and choose Open Anyway for Pocket Sync.
4. Confirm the prompt. macOS may ask you to authenticate.

The exception applies to Pocket Sync. If macOS reports malware or says the app
is damaged, stop and get a fresh copy from the publisher.
